Alternate history

The war that never happened.

A 1990s that never was. In the Republic of Parsava, a fictional country on the Gulf, the oil, the radars and the television all answer to Marshal Rashid al-Ghazi, a foreign-born strongman ruling Persian soil.

When his Guard fired on the protest squares, the crew of one battleship stopped taking orders. They took the Simorgh, her gunship and every mixtape aboard, and went to war with the regime that trained them. The state news calls them pirates. The villages cheer when they fly over.

Spring to the third winter.

Each campaign is one continuous, hand-authored theater. The war carries over: what you take in spring is who fights beside you in winter.

The doctrine

Fly the war. Never command it.

Desert Strike's cockpit welded to a Command & Conquer economy, and the helicopter is the only cursor you get.

Built block by block

You fly the base in

There is no build menu. Ferry the work crew, sling the block tarps off the Simorgh's cargo yard, and watch the building physically go up. Once it stands, its production runs itself.

Every wall on the beachhead was flown in under your rotors.

One cockpit

You never order a unit

The ground war is autonomous. Barracks replace their own dead, war factories roll their own columns, and both armies march on authored roads. You change the war by what you carry, what you destroy and who you bring home.

Destructible

Everything important breaks

Every building, bridge, ship and tower comes apart for real, and the rubble stays where it fell. Drop the only causeway across the strait and you live with the map you made.

The living war

It runs whether you show up or not.

Weather

Weather fights too

Fog seas in the northern valleys, rain on the strait, dust walls on a live wind field and ground blizzards in the third winter. None of it is a skybox.

The coast

A war on the water

Tankers keep their lanes while the shore burns. The sea floats the fleet, stops bullets, and obeys the tide.

Day and night

The war doesn't stop at dusk

Tracer, rocket trails and burning palms over a river town at night, with searchlights sweeping for you.

Oil country

Everything is full of fuel

Put a Hellfire into the tank farm and the fire runs tank to tank. Torched derricks burn for hours.

Towns

Towns that live

Lit windows and parked cars at dusk. Every town was a living place before the war reached it, and traffic still runs its real roads.

The gun run

Rockets, gun, Hellfires

Hydras for the soft targets, the chin gun for the infantry, Hellfires for the armor and the SAMs.
