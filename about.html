The studio

Reformat Games.

Reformat Games is an independent studio with a soft spot for the machines and genres the industry left behind: cartridge-era strike games, cassette mixtapes, and action movies that play it ninety percent straight.

PERSIAN STRIKE: RETRIBUTION is our love letter to all three: a Desert Strike-style helicopter war, rebuilt with a living battlefield and a base you raise block by block. It is in early access for Windows and Linux PC, and the war grows with every update.
